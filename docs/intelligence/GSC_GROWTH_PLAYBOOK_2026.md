## Honest math (do not market as guarantee)

- **Million+ impressions** are a long-game outcome of architecture + indexing + backlinks + time — **not** a Month 1–3 deliverable from content alone.
- Months 1–3 should deliver: industrial silo architecture, GIDC coverage, RFQ cost tool, stronger internal links, crawlable inventory Google can discover.
- Ranking wins concentrate on **low–mid KD industrial long-tails** and **GIDC/estate locality**, not winning “Software Development Company India” head-term wars.
