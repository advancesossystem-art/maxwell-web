## 2. MARKET STRUCTURE (HOW THE BOARD ACTUALLY WORKS)

Research shows **nine overlapping arenas**, not one competitor set:

1. **Local generalist agencies** — fight on city keywords + price + WhatsApp.
2. **Premium Indian product/engineering houses** — fight on Clutch, AWS/Azure partners, global clients.
3. **Manufacturing ERP/MES product vendors** — fight on modules, go-live speed, GST, shop floor.
4. **OT / Industry 4.0 integrators** — fight on PLC/SCADA/MES + Siemens/Rockwell partnerships.
5. **Enterprise digital engineering (LTTS, Accenture Industry X)** — fight on Fortune accounts; not Maxwell’s ICP but set buyer language.
6. **Industrial AI startups** — fight on vision QC, PdM, edge sensors; raise buyer expectations for “AI”.
7. **ERP platform partners (Odoo / SAP B1 / ERPNext)** — fight on partner badges + migration from Tally.
8. **Manufacturing marketing agencies** — fight on RFQ SEO, IndiaMart displacement, LinkedIn.
9. **Industrial website specialists** — fight on catalogues, dealer portals, export pages, Next.js/WordPress.

**Strategic implication for Maxwell:** The winning position is the **wedge between #1, #3, #8, and #9** — local Gujarat manufacturing access + custom digital engineering (website → dealer portal → ERP/CRM → AI) as one owned stack, without needing SAP Gold or Siemens System House status.
